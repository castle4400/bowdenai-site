Enterprise scale

From strategy to customer behaviour.

For a 24,000-customer EMEA population, I designed a three-layer Generative AI training motion: relationship-led engagement, AI-enabled scale and partner conversion—governed by real utilisation and paid-conversion gates.

Earlier attempts had stalled on data quality and internal buy-in. The answer was not a larger campaign; it was an operating model that connected account-team trust to partner delivery capacity with explicit rules.

The results supported a 4,255-place next-phase capacity plan. Relationship-led and scale-led motions work together when governance is disciplined and reporting is honest.

The control boundary

Autonomy should expand. Accountability should never disappear.

Most organisations already have AI in production. Far fewer have redesigned authority around systems that can decide and act.

Building Jeeves exposed the quiet failures a policy document misses: checks that report green without biting, role separation that exists only on a diagram, and autonomous behaviour whose confidence is not evidence.
