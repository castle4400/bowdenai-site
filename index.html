Applied AI & governance

Autonomy can grow. Accountability must remain clear.

Working with agentic systems has reinforced a practical lesson: good AI governance starts with knowing what an agent may do, what evidence it needs, and where a person must remain accountable.

That means respecting data boundaries, evaluating outputs, limiting permissions, documenting decisions and designing approval into the workflow. These are practitioner observations from personal experimentation, not claims of formal board or regulatory assurance.
